Pig's Gotta Fly plans VFR flights in the Athinai FIR, from the route on the map to the papers you take to the aircraft.

ROUTE
- Draw the route on a map with the Greek VFR chart data: aerodromes, VFR reporting points and the published VFR routes with their altitudes, controlled airspace, restricted and danger areas.
- Drag a point to move it, drag a leg's middle to add a point, press and hold for a point's options.
- Each leg shows its course, distance and planned altitude; the app proposes VFR altitudes from the routes and airspace.

NAVLOG
- Navigation log with winds aloft for each leg: true and magnetic course, wind correction, heading with your compass card, ground speed, leg times and fuel.
- Taxi times, block and flight times, sunset at the destination.

AIRCRAFT, FUEL, WEIGHT AND BALANCE, PERFORMANCE
- A hangar of your aircraft with their weighing reports, stations, envelopes, fuel tanks and performance tables.
- Trip fuel, reserves and fuel on board, and the loading sheet with the centre of gravity on the envelope at take-off and landing.
- Take-off and landing distances for the aerodromes' runways and the forecast conditions.

BEFORE THE FLIGHT
- Aerodrome operating hours for the times of the flight.
- NOTAMs that concern the route and its altitudes, as a briefing you can print.
- METAR and TAF of the aerodromes along the route.

PAPERWORK
- The ICAO flight plan, ready to file from the app.
- Form 731 and the General Declaration, with your passengers' details, signed on screen and sent by e-mail.
- Every document prints on paper with AirPrint.

Your flights, aircraft and passengers are saved to your account and are the same on the web at pigsgottafly.com, on iPhone and on iPad.
